## Core Principle

Lit Alerts product matching in this workspace is a two-phase workflow:

1. Broad identity discovery.
2. Narrow evidence filtering.

Use statewide search, exact brand identity, category filters, and one durable family token first. Only apply stricter matching after the listing cluster clearly identifies the product family.
